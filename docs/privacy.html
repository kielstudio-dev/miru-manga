Privacy Policy

Miru Manga — Independent Reader Application

Developer: Kiel Studio Devs • Effective Date: October 20, 2026

📖 1. Introduction

Welcome to Miru Manga, an application created and maintained by Kiel Studio Devs ("we", "us", or "our"). We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our readers.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, handles, and protects any information when you read comics, manhwa, manga, and use our cloud synchronization features.

Summary Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertising networks. Free reading is available 100% anonymously without forced registration.

⚡ 2. Independent Media Player Legal Model

Miru Manga operates as an independent media reader tool. The core architecture is designed to protect both reader privacy and digital rights:

  • No Content Hosted: Miru Manga does not host, distribute, or store copyrighted comic files, scans, or media on its own servers.
  • User-Provided & Community Content: All local archives (.cbz, .zip, .cbr) and third-party community repository extensions run client-side on the user's private device.
  • Local Cache: Any temporary page images extracted while reading are stored strictly within the app's local sandbox cache on your device and can be purged at any time in Settings.

🔍 3. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection (Data Minimization):

  • Anonymous / Guest Readers (Default): You are not required to provide your name, phone number, or email to use the application. An anonymous random UUID is generated locally to allow seamless bookmarking and reading progress tracking without identifying who you are.
  • Google Sign-In / Account Linking (Optional for Lifetime Pro): When you choose to link your Google account to protect your Pro purchase or restore your library across multiple devices, we receive basic authentication details via official Google OAuth:
    • Your Google account email address.
    • Your public display name and avatar (solely for displaying your profile status in Settings).
    • A unique authentication identifier provided by Google.
  • Reading History & Bookmarks: Titles you bookmark and chapters you mark as read are synchronized to our secure, encrypted cloud database (Supabase) strictly to enable cross-device synchronization for your personal library.

💳 4. Payments & Billing

Miru Manga offers an optional one-time purchase ("Lifetime Pro"). All financial transactions are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing.

  • Kiel Studio Devs does not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses.
  • Google Play handles all billing security, fraud prevention, and receipts in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • We only receive an anonymous purchase token to verify your Lifetime Pro status and grant permanent ad removal and features.

🔗 5. Third-Party Service Providers

Our application integrates with reputable, privacy-compliant infrastructure providers:

  • Google Play Services: Used for secure native Android authentication and In-App Purchases.
  • Supabase: An open-source, SOC2-compliant PostgreSQL cloud backend used to store encrypted user bookmarks and reading progress.
  • Expo / EAS: Used for distributing Over-The-Air app patches and bug fixes.

🛡️ 6. Children's Privacy

Miru Manga is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will delete the data from our servers.

In accordance with Google Play Store guidelines, mature (18+) community content is blocked by default and requires explicit age verification within the app's settings.

🗑️ 7. Your Data Rights & Account Deletion

You have complete ownership and control of your reading data and account at all times:

  • In-App One-Click Account & Data Deletion: Inside the Miru Manga app, go to Settings ➡️ Cloud Backup & Sync ➡️ Delete Data or Settings ➡️ Legal & Compliance ➡️ Delete Account & Cloud Data. This immediately and permanently deletes your cloud account, bookmarks, and server records.
  • Sign Out & Local Mode: You can disconnect your Google account at any time in Settings ➡️ Cloud Backup & Sync to revert to an offline guest session.
  • Export & Portability: You can export your entire library, history, and custom repositories into a portable JSON backup file at any time.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the app, you may submit a formal data deletion request by emailing us at kielstudio.dev@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Please specify your account email address. Requests are fulfilled and confirmed within 48 hours.

⚖️ 8. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Safe Harbor

Miru Manga operates in strict compliance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512). The software functions as an independent, neutral client application (similar to a web browser or media player). We do not host, control, or own third-party comic content or community repository indexes.

If you are a copyright owner or authorized representative and believe that content accessible via third-party extensions infringes upon your copyright, you may send a formal DMCA takedown notice to our Designated Copyright Agent at kielstudio.dev@gmail.com with the required statutory elements. We respond expeditiously to legitimate notices.

💬 9. In-App User Support & Problem Reports

When you submit an issue report, bug report, or extension suggestion through the app's Settings ➡️ Help & User Support modal, your message and optional contact email are transmitted securely to our developer backend for the sole purpose of resolving technical problems and improving the app experience. Feedback data is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
